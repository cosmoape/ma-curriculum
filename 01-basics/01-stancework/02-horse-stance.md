# Horse Stance

## Purpose
The kung fu classic. Horse stance (馬步) is the base for the jab, so its function is equally versatile. It allows us to jab, close distance, change level, and feint. It's basically a wider and lower version of the neutral stance.

## Instruction
1. From neutral stance, take a step forward with your front foot, without changing its angle and without shifting your weight forward.
2. Your weight should simply drop down, as if you're sitting down
