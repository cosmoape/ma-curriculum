# Horse Stance

## Overview
The kung fu classic. Named after the shape your legs make when riding a horse, horse stance (馬步) is the base for the jab, and its function is equally versatile. It's basically a wider and lower version of the neutral stance.

## Instruction
1. From neutral stance, take a step forward with your front foot, without changing your foot's angle.
2. Your weight should remain 50-50 and simply drop down right in between your feet.

## Checklist
- [ ] Your weight and upper body remain right in the middle of your feet. Don't lean off the horse.
- [ ] You should be able to push off your front foot again to return quickly back into neutral stance. If you can't, you're probably leaning or shifting too much weight into your front foot.

## Tips
- Imagine a weight or plumb line hanging down from your crotch, and dropping that weight towards the ground.
- Imagine a fake-out or performing a jab step in basketball or tag. In and out.
- Imagine trying to push a rug with your foot - it's almost more of a kick than a step in the conventional sense of walking.
