# Reverse Stance

## Overview
As its name suggests, the reverse stance is almost the opposite of the front stance, turning our hips and shoulders towards the back, but not quite to the point of facing backwards. Reverse stance drives your lead hook and outside slip. 

## Instruction
1. From front stance, pivot and push off of your front foot this time, flaring your front heel out and slightly forward.
2. At the same time, pivot and plant your rear heel flat on the ground, and shift your weight 70-30 onto your rear foot, with your head place roughly over your rear heel.
3. This motion should drive your hips and shoulders to turn slightly past 90 degrees, so that your chest now faces to the side and slightly backwards.

## Checklist
- [ ] As always, you should feel balanced, able to bounce, and come back to neutral stance.
- [ ] Your feet flip like a switch - your front heel flips up and out at the sime time as your rear heel plants down, and your weight shifts over at the same time.
